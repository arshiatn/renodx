These headers are the SDK's include folder as Nsight Graphics 2026.4.0 installs it
(SDKs/NsightGraphicsSDK/0.9.2/include), unmodified: only NGFX_GPUTrace_D3D12.h,
NGFX_GraphicsCapture_D3D12.h and the headers they include. The Total War helper
(src/games/twattila/dlaa_helper) uses them to mark its frames for Nsight Graphics'
GPU Trace and Graphics Capture; without Nsight Graphics the calls do nothing.
Nsight Graphics itself is not part of this repository or of the mod.
