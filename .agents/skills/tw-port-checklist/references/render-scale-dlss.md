# Render scale, upscalers, jitter, motion vectors

## 1. Scale checklist

Run it at 100, 67, 50 and 33%, with a cold start at the low scale and after a live change. A proof at 100% says nothing below it.

Files are in `twattila/` unless named; WH3's scale code is `twwarhammer3/taa_dlss.hpp`.

- **Sizes** (`frame_history::AspectSize`, `AspectScale`): whole aspect units; the game's rounding; screen against viewport registers; overscan; a check frame per new size (WH3's `ScaleProof`); forget a size when its source goes.
- **Viewports and scissors** (`anti_aliasing.hpp`, `particle_layer.hpp`): scaled at 0,0 for display-size and power-of-two targets; scissors back after the tone mapper; `dlaa_bridge::Evaluate` takes the viewport, not the texture.
- **uv to NDC** (`view_share.hpp`'s patch kinds): in the game's shaders and in our replacements (patch order).
- **Level chains** (`ssr_hiz.hpp`, `depth_levels.hpp`): mips, Hi-Z, depth levels, bloom, exposure, AO downsample; half-size targets read whole.
- **Overscan readers** (`ssao_constants_slot`): AO, heat haze, secondary LUT, DOF, distortion, fog of war.
- **Pixel tolerances** (`screen_space_shadows.hpp`): Bend over the share, SSS softness, reflection blur, the stored hit distance, the flare occlusion count, trail texels.
- **The game's own scale terms**: sharpening, the TAA's weight, LOD and culling at load, its own DLSS (section 3).
- **Samplers** (`texture_lod_bias.hpp`): by kind; main view only (`frame_history::MainViewport`).
- **Our effects** (`frame_history::effect_share`, `EffectSteps`): AO resolution x share, Light Bounce one step finer per halving, reflection rays up a step, a 1:1 march at or below half, guides and Depth Levels over the rendered corner, debug views stretched.
- **After DLSS** (`anti_aliasing.hpp`): markers need a display-size depth; particles; UI.
- **Jitter** (`anti_aliasing::Update`): phase count, amplitude and sign per method and scale; FG's camera without jitter.
- **States**: menus, upscaler not running, registers set by a replaced pass.
- **Tools**: traces at 100% and 50% with pass and index diffs; [scale_census.py](../scripts/scale_census.py) over the whole dump (procedure and limits in [bringup.md](bringup.md) Phase 0); `scale_audit.py` (it lists dispatches but passes nothing: read each CS's mapping); `wh3_batch5/p9_ndc_trace.py`; WARP with the picture in the top-left of a 2x texture against 100%.
- **Done means**: every screen-space pass swept at Ultra Performance (33%) in the campaign and in a battle, and the census's BUG rows fixed or listed with the one trace that settles each.

## 2. Motion-vector checklist

- **Census**: class every G-buffer VS by what its velocity output holds (WH3: a table by VS hash). Fix by token copy (`dxbc::AddPreviousPosition`: the same bits by construction); swap a PS only with a listed VS and an R16G16 target.
- **Camera**: per frame, without jitter, snapshot at the evaluation.
- **Skinned units**: repeated bone packs within a frame (section 3), keys per frame, the campaign's placement scale.
- **Rigid placed rows**: `SV_InstanceID`, repacked streams, radius x placement scale.
- **Wind**: only time is last frame's.
- **Usual gaps**: deformed cloth, sails, ropes; banners (rows only, CPU-moved buffers); water (one history texture per slot); glowing and dissolving weapons (PS without the velocity output); particles, heat haze.
- **Proofs**: paused, row on against off; WARP stream-output bits; the Motion Debug View per class; cost inside one run.
